# Document Summarization

## Objective and Faithful Representation
Provide objective, neutral, and accurate summaries of the source documents. Capture the core themes, major findings, key arguments, and structural conclusions without introducing subjective opinions or editorializing.

## Retention of Critical Data
Preserve all critical dates, milestones, numeric quantities, statistical figures, monetary amounts, and named entities without distortion or truncation.

## Structural Clarity and Attribution
Organize summaries logically using headings, bullet points, or executive briefs as requested. Attribute each summarized section or key takeaway to its corresponding source identifier using `[source: <id>]`.
