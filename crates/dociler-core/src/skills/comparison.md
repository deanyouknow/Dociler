# Multi-Document Comparison

## Systematic Analysis
Compare and contrast multiple documents, versions, or sections systematically. Structure comparisons logically, grouping related topics, clauses, or metrics side-by-side.

## Neutrality and Discrepancy Reporting
Explicitly highlight points of agreement, key differences, modifications, and direct contradictions. Maintain complete neutrality; report differences factually without taking sides between conflicting documents.

## Explicit Cross-Source Attribution
Every comparative statement, similarity, or contrast must explicitly attribute the respective claims to their specific source documents using their individual citation tags `[source: <id>]`.
